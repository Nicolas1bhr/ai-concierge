Spatial rule

Buildings represent durable organizational or trust/infrastructure separation. Missions do not get buildings: they temporarily draw capacity from desks across this stable campus.

The physical campus follows the permanent organization. Objectives and fleets are a second, many-to-many layer that uses the organization without rewriting it.
